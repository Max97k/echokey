# AKeyboard v0.5.0 (versionCode 4)

Google Play Internal Testing build `v0.5.0 (4)`.

## What's new

- **10 languages localization**: full UI translation support for English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, German, Spanish, French, Indonesian, Japanese, Korean, and Brazilian Portuguese.
- **Android 13+ Per-App Language Preferences**: auto-generated locale config (`generateLocaleConfig = true`) allowing per-app language selection directly from Android System Settings.
- **Unified language bundle**: `enableSplit = false` ensures all translation resources remain available offline without downloading language split APKs.
- **Brand & visual update**: refreshed app launcher icons and Play Store visual assets.

## 更新內容（繁體中文）

- **支援 10 種語言在地化**：完整支援英文、繁體中文、簡體中文、德文、西班牙文、法文、印尼文、日文、韓文、巴西葡萄牙文介面。
- **Android 13+ 應用程式獨立語言設定**：自動生成 Locale Config，可在系統設定中直接指定 AKeyboard 專用語系。
- **完整語系資源打包**：關閉語言分割套件，確保所有語系離線即時可用。
- **品牌圖示更新**：更新應用程式啟動圖示與 Google Play 商店視覺資源。
